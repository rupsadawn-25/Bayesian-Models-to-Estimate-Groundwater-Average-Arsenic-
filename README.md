# Project Title: [Bayesian Hierarchical Models to Estimate Groundwater Average Arsenic in Mandari Village, Bangladesh]

This project reports the results of a simulation study conducted to estimate the average groundwater arsenic level. Minding the hazards caused by high level of arsenic to the environment we conducted this based on the models used in section 2 and 3 of paper by Banerjee (2025).  This study applies a Bayesian finite population framework to estimate the mean and total arsenic concentration of tube wells in Mandari Village, Bangladesh. Bayesian models corresponding to Horvitz–Thompson estimation are used to investigate the relationship between model-based and design-based inference. Posterior distributions of finite population parameters are obtained using Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods, allowing direct probabilistic inference on unknown population quantities.
